# Welcome to the TakaTime Wiki! 

TakaTime is a blazingly fast, privacy-focused coding time tracker. It works just like WakaTime, but with one major difference: **You own your data.** Instead of sending your coding activity to a third-party server, TakaTime stores everything locally in SQLite and syncs to your own personal MongoDB database.

Use the sidebar on the right (or the links below) to navigate the documentation.
